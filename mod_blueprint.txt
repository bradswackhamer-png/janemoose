[KEY ARCHITECTURAL MECHANICS & LOGIC]

1. TRACTOR VEHICLE & PHYSICS (JaneMooseTractorEntity):
   - Steer via A/D keys (smooth yaw turning rate, no strafing).
   - Throttle via W/S keys (smooth forward & reverse acceleration).
   - Speed governance: Travels at 100% normal speed when transport/unhitched; throttled to 50% working speed when an attachment is lowered and actively working.
   - Wheels: True 16-faceted round cylinders. Wheel spin animation only advances while vehicle is in motion; wheels lock when parked.
   - Driver HUD: Standard horse/animal health bar removed by overriding rideable entity attributes so no false hearts appear.
   - Fuel Mechanics: Dual-fuel engine supporting both Diesel (`janemoose:diesel_canister`, `refinery:diesel_bucket`, `#c:diesel`) and Ethanol biofuel (`refinery:ethanol_bucket`, `#c:ethanol`, `#c:fuels/ethanol`). Consumes fuel based on engine load; stalls when empty. Refueled via right-click or Tractor GUI (+1,000 mB per canister/bucket, returning empty buckets).

2. ATTACHMENTS & TIERS (JaneMooseAttachmentEntity):
   - Placement: Holding any attachment item (Cultivator, Planter, Harvester) and right-clicking any block immediately places the 3D entity on the ground.
   - Hitch Mechanism: 3-point hitch connects when tractor reverses up to an attachment and right-clicks with Wrench.
   - Mode States:
     * Raised (Transport Mode): Implements lifted 8px above ground; tools disengage; no tractor speed penalty; visual animation pauses.
     * Lowered (Working Mode): Implements touch ground; PTO engages; tractor speed set to 50%; tool active.
   - Cultivator: Tills grass/dirt into fertile farmland (matches Hoe functionality); includes anti-trample soil preservation so tractor tires never trample tilled soil. No internal inventory needed.
   - Planter: Real-world row units; contains 9-slot seed inventory; auto-plants seeds (wheat, carrot, potato, beetroot) onto farmland as tractor drives over.
   - Harvester: Combine header with rotating 4-bat reel, sickle cutterbar, and cross-auger; breaks mature crops, replants seeds automatically, and routes gathered harvest into internal 9-slot grain tank.
   - Swath Widths: Wood = 1 block; Iron = 3 blocks; Diamond = 5 blocks.

3. GUI & SCREEN RESILIENCE:
   - AttachmentMenu & AttachmentScreen: Buffer sends attachment entity ID and slot count. Uses clean if-else logic (no synthetic enum-mapping switch classes) and resilient client/server slot alignment to prevent ClassNotFoundException / ZipException when hot-reloading.
   - TractorScreen: Dedicated hopper button routes to ACTION_OPEN_ATTACHMENT_GUI on server, cleanly opening AttachmentScreen.

4. 3D MODELS (TractorModel & AttachmentModel) & Z-FIGHTING RESOLUTIONS:
   - Tractor: Authentically proportioned John Deere 4020 silhouette. Detailed 16-sided faceted wheels, front stacked suitcase weights, clean front nose, radiator honeycomb mesh, dual chrome headlights, graphite smokestack with rain flapper cap, cyclone pre-cleaner dome, vinyl operator seat, ROPS canopy sunshade, and 3-point hitch lift arms.
   - Rear Chassis & Axle Decoupling: Frame rails end cleanly at Z=6.95F; rear axle starts at Z=7.0F and runs to Z=10.0F. No double-box internal overlap, zero coplanar top, bottom, or back faces.
   - Platform Floor Deck: Diamond-plate platform extends back to Z=9.8F at Y=-5.8F, resting cleanly above the axle with no coplanar Z-fighting.
   - Inset Wheel Rims: Diagonal star rim boxes are inset by 0.02F in X (rim_diag & rrim_diag) so outer end caps are not coplanar, eliminating 100% of rim flickering.
   - Grille & Headlights: Removed conflicting nose cowl and overlapping box; the radiator grille sits cleanly at the front with honeycomb mesh, yellow border, leaping moose badge, and unobstructed round headlights.
   - Hitch Geometry: Drawbar and PTO stub shaft offset to Z=10.05F, cleanly separating them from the axle back face at Z=10.0F.

5. 2K PHOTOREALISTIC TEXTURING & LABPBR POM SHADER SUPPORT:
   - 2048x2048 (2K) diffuse textures baked with ambient occlusion and brushed metal highlights.
   - LabPBR 1.3 Normal & Height POM maps (_n.png) and Specular Roughness/Metallic maps (_s.png).
   - Decal Preservation: Painted decals and yellow stripe ("JANE MOOSE 4020") have flat height (255) and flat tangent normals (128, 128, 255), completely preventing POM raymarching stepping artifacts so text renders vector-crisp under all shader packs.
   - Rich POM 3D depth enabled on tactile elements: tire lugs, honeycomb grille, louvers, diamond plate, and chrome bezels.

6. DYNAMIC HEADLIGHT ILLUMINATION & HOTKEY SYSTEM:
   - Controls: Assignable hotkey in Controls > Key Binds under 'Jane Moose Machinery' (default key [L]) and dedicated button on Tractor Dashboard GUI.
   - Illumination Origin: Dynamic server-side light source (Level 15 invisible Blocks.LIGHT) placed 1.85m in front of tractor along the yaw forward vector at Y+0.85 (headlight height), illuminating a 15-block radius directly in front of the grille.
   - Adaptive Placement: Automatically checks open air, water (with LightBlock.WATERLOGGED support), or elevated terrain so illumination never clips inside solid terrain.
   - Full Lifecycle Cleanup: Light block moves smoothly as tractor drives, clears immediately when toggled OFF, unhitched, discarded, picked up, or unloaded, leaving zero phantom light blocks in the world.

7. FERMENTATION BARREL & ETHANOL BIOFUEL LOOP:
   - 3D Rounded Block Geometry: Custom 8-sided rounded oak staves with bulging bilge, dual riveted iron hoops, recessed top/bottom chime heads, and front brass tap.
   - Fermentation Rate: Converts 1 Wheat -> 1,000 mB (1 Bucket) of Ethanol over exactly 1,200 ticks (60.0 seconds = 1 real-life minute), trickle-filling +5 mB every 6 ticks into an 8,000 mB internal tank.
   - Dual Extraction: Right-click barrel with an Empty Bucket to instantly draw a bucket, or place buckets in GUI Slot 1 to auto-fill into Slot 2. Full hopper/pipe automation via WorldlyContainer.
