## 🚜 Quick Start Guide

1. **Craft or Spawn the Tractor**: Place the Jane Moose 4020 tractor on flat ground using the tractor item.
2. **Fuel It Up**: Right-click the tractor with a **Diesel Canister** (+1,000 mB) or diesel bucket.
3. **Mount & Drive**: Right-click with an empty hand to mount. Steer with `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`.
4. **Night Farming (Headlights)**: Press `[L]` or open the dashboard (`[C]`) to activate torch-level front headlights.
5. **Hitch Implements**: Place a Cultivator, Planter, or Harvester behind the tractor, then reverse up to it to hitch automatically.
6. **Engage Equipment**: Press `[H]` to lower the implement to the ground, and `[X]` to engage PTO power.
