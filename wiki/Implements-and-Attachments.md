# 🌾 Implements & Attachments

Jane Moose includes three authentic agricultural implements across three tiers (**Wood**, **Iron**, and **Diamond**), designed for high-efficiency field work.

---

## 🛠️ Attachment Tiers & Working Swaths

| Implement | Tier I (Wood) | Tier II (Iron) | Tier III (Diamond) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Field Cultivator** | 1-block swath | 2-block swath | 3-block swath |
| **Row-Crop Planter** | 1-block (9 slots) | 2-block (18 slots) | 3-block (27 slots) |
| **Combine Harvester** | 1-block (9 slots) | 2-block (18 slots) | 3-block (27 slots) |

---

## 1. 🌱 Spring-Tooth Field Cultivator
* **Function**: Tills grass, dirt, and coarse dirt into fertile farmland.
* **Anti-Trample Soil Preservation**: Tractor wheels are governed to never trample or compact tilled farmland while an implement is hitched.
* **32x32 Visuals (v1.0.3)**:
  * Top A-frame 3-point hitch coupler.
  * Structural rectangular toolbar beam (wood, Jane Moose green, or diamond armored).
  * Curved spring-steel C-shanks with top coil mounts.
  * Hardened duckfoot sweep plow points slicing through the ground.

---

## 2. 🌽 Multi-Row Crop Planter
* **Function**: Automatically sows seeds onto tilled farmland as the tractor drives over.
* **Supported Crops**: Wheat, Carrot, Potato, Beetroot, Pumpkin Seeds, Melon Seeds, and Torchflower.
* **Internal Inventory**: Right-click the planter or open via Tractor Dashboard hopper button to load seed stacks.
* **32x32 Visuals (v1.0.3)**:
  * Tapered row-unit seed hoppers with latching lids.
  * Ribbed flexible seed drop tubes.
  * Double-disk furrow opener coulters.
  * Trailing rubber V-closing press wheels.

---

## 3. 🌾 Combine Grain Harvester
* **Function**: Breaks mature crops, replants seed automatically, and gathers harvest into an internal grain tank.
* **Internal Grain Tank**: Collects wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots, and excess seeds.
* **32x32 Visuals (v1.0.3)**:
  * Rotating 4-bat pickup reel with end star spiders.
  * Horizontal gathering cross-auger drum.
  * Sharp lower sickle cutterbar with rock guards and reciprocating knife sections.
  * Outer pointed torpedo divider snouts.

---

## 🚜 Hitching & Modes
* **Deploy**: Right-click the ground with any implement item to place its 3D entity.
* **Hitch**: Reverse the tractor up to the implement drawbar to connect automatically.
* **Transport Mode (Raised)**: Press `[H]` to lift implement 8px above ground; tractor travels at 100% normal speed.
* **Working Mode (Lowered)**: Press `[H]` to lower implement to the ground; tractor speed is governed to 50% working speed and PTO engages.
* **Power Toggle**: Press `[X]` to turn implement active power ON or OFF.
