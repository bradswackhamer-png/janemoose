# 🚜 Tractor Operation & Driving

The **Jane Moose Model 4020** is an authentic rideable diesel farm tractor engineered for realistic agricultural workflows.

---

## 🚗 Driving Mechanics
* **Throttle**: Press `W` to accelerate forward smoothly; press `S` for reverse and braking.
* **Steering**: Press `A` and `D` to steer. Turning rate is proportional to forward velocity with a realistic turning circle.
* **Speed Governance**:
  * **Transport Speed (100%)**: Active when unhitched or when rear implement is in **Raised (Transport)** position.
  * **Working Speed (50%)**: Automatically engaged when an implement is **Lowered** and working the field.
* **Clean Driver HUD**: False horse/animal health bars are suppressed so no hearts clutter your screen.

---

## 💡 Dynamic Headlights
* **Torch-Level Illumination (Light Level 15)**: Dual chrome front grille headlights project a 15-block illumination beam forward.
* **Toggle Controls**: Press `[L]` while driving or click the **Lights: ON / OFF** button on the tractor dashboard.
* **Smart Lifecycle**: Light blocks track smoothly as you drive, avoid clipping into solid terrain, support waterlogged blocks, and despawn instantly when lights are toggled off or the vehicle is unhitched.

---

## 📋 Tractor Dashboard GUI
* Press `[C]` while mounted or sneak-right-click the tractor from outside.
* Monitor live fuel gauge ($10,000\text{ mB}$ max capacity).
* View engine load, operating status, and hitched implement stats.
* Quick-toggle headlights and open implement inventories (Seed Hopper / Grain Tank) with one click.
